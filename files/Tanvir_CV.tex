%----------------------------------------------------------------- Research
\section{Research Project}
\entry{GreenRAN: Machine Learning for Base-Station Sleep Control}{\href{https://github.com/siyam-05/GreenRAN}{github.com/siyam-05/GreenRAN}}
      {Independent research project, Python and NumPy}{2026}
\vspace{-1pt}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Simulated one macro cell and 12 small cells to study when a small cell can sleep to save energy without dropping traffic.
  \item Implemented K-means traffic clustering, Gaussian anomaly detection and a Deep Q-Network from scratch in NumPy, including
        backpropagation and the Adam optimizer, checked against numerical gradients and unit tests.
  \item Tuned all methods on 56 validation days and evaluated once on 112 unseen test days over 5 random seeds:
        \textbf{31.3\%} energy saved while carrying \textbf{99.92\%} of traffic, reaching \textbf{93\%} of the saving possible with perfect foresight.
  \item Reported negative results openly: a carefully tuned load-aware rule matched the agent (31.8\%), and the agent became stable
        only when given the K-means traffic types.
\end{itemize}
